# Privacy Policy

Last updated: 8 September 2026

GearMate is an app for finding beginner-friendly guitars, basses, amps and
multi-effects. This policy describes what the app collects, why, and who it is
shared with.

## We do not ask you to create an account

GearMate has no sign-up and no login. We do not collect your name, email
address, phone number or postal address.

## What the app collects

**An install identifier.** The first time you open GearMate, the app generates a
random identifier and stores it on your device. It is sent with each request so
the server can keep your cart and your saved preferences separate from other
people's. It is not derived from your device, your Apple Account or any
advertising identifier, and it is not shared with advertisers. Deleting the app
deletes it.

**What you type into the consultation chat.** When you describe what you are
looking for, the text is sent to our server so it can find matching instruments.

**Your stated preferences.** Budget, preferred categories, genres and uses, if
you enter them.

**Your cart and your orders.** GearMate does not process real payments; ordering
an instrument in the app is a simulation and no money changes hands. The
contents are stored so the app can show them back to you.

**Subscription status.** If you subscribe to GearMate Pro, your subscription is
handled by RevenueCat, which records the purchase against an anonymous
identifier.

## Who we send it to

**Anthropic and OpenAI.** Consultation text is sent to Anthropic (Claude) to
understand your request, and to OpenAI to turn instrument descriptions into a
form that can be searched by meaning. Only the text of your request is sent. The
install identifier is not.

**RevenueCat.** Purchases and subscription status. RevenueCat receives an
anonymous identifier, not your name or email address.

**Apple.** If you subscribe, Apple processes the payment. We never see your
payment details.

We do not sell your data and we do not use it for advertising.

## How long we keep it

Cart contents, orders, saved preferences and consultation history are kept while
the install identifier exists. Deleting the app makes the data unreachable from
your device; you can ask us to delete it from the server using the contact
address below.

## Children

GearMate is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect
data from them.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top of this page changes with it.
