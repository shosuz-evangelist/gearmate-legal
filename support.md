# Support

GearMate Instruments helps you find guitars, basses, amps and effects by asking
in plain English.

## Contact

Questions, problems, or a request to delete your data:

**info@developeradvocate.jp**

We aim to reply within a few business days.

## Common questions

### Can I actually buy the instruments?

No. The instruments come from a catalog included with the app, not from a real
shop. Ordering is a simulation: no real order is placed, no payment is taken,
and nothing ships.

### What does GearMate Pro cover?

Unlimited consultations and recommendations. Browsing the catalog stays free.
Pro is $4.99 per month or $39.99 per year.

### How do I cancel?

Open the Settings app on your device, tap your name, then Subscriptions, and
select GearMate. Cancelling stops the next renewal; you keep Pro until the
current period ends.

### How do I restore a purchase?

Open the Ask tab and tap Restore Purchases on the subscription screen.

### The catalog will not load

The app needs a network connection to reach its server. If the problem
continues, write to the address above and say what you saw.
